About Rosebud Staffing Solutions

Created in 2023 with over 20 Years combined experience, Rosebud Staffing Solutions has been the trusted partner for healthcare facilities seeking exceptional nursing professionals. Our mission is to bridge the gap between skilled caregivers and the communities that need them.

We understand that quality healthcare depends on having the right people in the right place at the right time. That's why we've built a network of over 500 vetted, licensed professionals ready to serve.

Whether you need temporary coverage for a single shift or permanent placement for your growing facility, we're here to help you maintain the highest standards of patient care.
